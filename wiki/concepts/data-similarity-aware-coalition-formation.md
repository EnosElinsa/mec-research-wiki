# Data-Similarity-Aware Coalition Formation

A cooperative grouping rule in which users prefer coalition heads and peers with overlapping requested data, allowing one download to be cached and shared among several members. Utility balances successful acquisition against the head's distinct-data download cost and members' D2D forwarding roles.

[[du-2026-hierarchical-coalition-deployment]] combines user joining and similarity matching with Pareto-admissible merge and exchange operations under coalition capacity and reachability constraints. Its stable partition admits no improving operation from that defined set, including multilateral merge and exchange moves; this does not imply the globally best partition.
