# Narrowband Internet of Things

NB-IoT is a 3GPP low-power wide-area cellular technology that operates in a 180 kHz carrier and uses repeated narrowband physical channels to extend coverage for constrained devices. [[krishna-m-2026-multiuav-nbiot]] models NPRACH access, NPUSCH uplink data, and NPDSCH downlink signaling when hovering UAVs relay otherwise disconnected ground devices to a base station.
