# Passive Six-Dimensional Movable Antenna

A rigid UAV-mounted passive reflecting surface controlled through three-dimensional platform position, three-axis orientation, and per-element reflection phases. The reflecting elements retain fixed relative positions: the whole surface moves and rotates.

This distinguishes [[liu-2026-passive-6dma]] from element-level [[movable-antenna]] designs and from the active radiating/receiving [[six-dimensional-aerial-rotatable-antenna-array]] in [[wang-2026-6dara-cellfree]]. Its geometry also changes the [[angle-dependent-irs-effective-aperture]] seen by the incident and reflected paths.
