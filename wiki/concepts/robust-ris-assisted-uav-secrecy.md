# Robust RIS-Assisted UAV Secrecy

Robust RIS-assisted UAV secrecy couples UAV motion, legitimate transmit power, and passive RIS phases while protecting both uplink and downlink traffic against bounded uncertainty in cascaded eavesdropper channels. The objective is a weighted time-average worst-case secrecy rate rather than nominal channel performance.

[[li-2021-robust-ris-uav-secrecy]] uses the S-Procedure, SDR, SCA, Gaussian randomization, and alternating optimization for this design. The construction assumes perfect legitimate CSI, continuous RIS phases, one fixed-altitude UAV, one user, and one eavesdropper; it yields a simulation-based local design rather than a global or hardware-validated guarantee.
