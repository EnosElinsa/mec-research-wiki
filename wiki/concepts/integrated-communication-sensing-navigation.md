# Integrated Communication-Sensing-Navigation

A tri-functional waveform and spatial-control framework in which sensing estimates aerial directions, communication carries those estimates and user data, and the shared covariance budget continues to support target perception. It extends [[integrated-sensing-and-communication]] by treating sensing-derived state as navigation assistance for aerial users.

In [[lu-2026-icsn-beamforming]], navigation means delivery of angular localization information; navigation error, integrity, trajectory guidance, and 3-D position accuracy are not modeled. The concept therefore records a functional integration pattern, not evidence of a complete navigation system.
