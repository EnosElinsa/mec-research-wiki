# Underground Air-Soil Wireless Channel

An above-ground-to-buried link model that combines propagation through air, refraction loss at the air-soil interface, and attenuation through lossy soil. Soil depth, volumetric water content, clay fraction, and carrier frequency can dominate the received-power budget.

[[lin-2026-uav-wpucn-time-allocation]] uses this model for wireless energy delivery to underground devices and evaluates severe sensitivity to burial depth and water content. Its formulation assumes a common burial depth, vertical underground propagation, and a mineralogy-based complex-permittivity model; those assumptions should not be generalized to layered or infrastructure-rich soil.
