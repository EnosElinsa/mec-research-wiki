# Integrated Sensing, Communication, and Power Transfer

Integrated sensing, communication, and power transfer (ISCPT) uses a shared wireless infrastructure to deliver information, illuminate sensing targets, and energize low-power devices. It combines the service roles of [[integrated-sensing-and-communication|ISAC]] and [[simultaneous-wireless-information-and-power-transfer|SWIPT]], but introduces a three-way design tradeoff among communication interference, sensing geometry/echo strength, and harvested RF power.

[[lu-2026-multiuav-iscpt]] implements ISCPT with multiple aerial transmitters. It optimizes worst-user SINR, worst-target received sensing power, and worst-user harvested energy through learned static 3-D deployment and power control.
