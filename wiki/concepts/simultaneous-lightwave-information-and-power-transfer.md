# Simultaneous Lightwave Information and Power Transfer

Simultaneous lightwave information and power transfer (SLIPT) uses one optical waveform for information detection and photovoltaic energy harvesting. Receiver architectures can separate AC and DC components, switch between decoding and harvesting in time, split received optical power, or combine time switching with power splitting.

[[kamatchi-2025-slipt-uav-fso]] analyzes these choices over a [[ground-to-uav-fso-channel]] and reports the hybrid time-switching/power-splitting receiver as its strongest energy-harvesting option. The result is limited to an analytical single-link model with idealized receiver branches: splitter loss, circuit power, battery dynamics, propulsion demand, and a powered second-hop relay are not evaluated.
