# 3-D Frequency Reuse

3-D frequency reuse generalizes planar cellular reuse to volumetric aerial networks. Instead of hexagonal cells tiling a plane, the coverage volume is partitioned into polyhedral cells so co-channel reuse can be reasoned about in all three spatial dimensions.

[[prabhath-not-in-parse-3d-space-spectrum-utilization]] uses truncated-octahedron tessellation for 3-D UAV-enabled cellular networks. A target UAV-gNB cell has 14 first-tier co-channel interferers, and the analysis studies how cell radius, reuse distance, channel loading, and fading affect [[spectrum-utilization-efficiency]] and link spectral efficiency. This is the spectrum-management counterpart to [[drone-cell-3d-placement]], which places aerial base stations for coverage.
