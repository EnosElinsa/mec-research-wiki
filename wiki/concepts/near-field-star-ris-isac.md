# Near-Field STAR-RIS ISAC

Near-field STAR-RIS ISAC uses spherical-wave propagation through a large simultaneous transmitting and reflecting surface to focus communication and sensing energy by both angle and range. This distance-domain degree of freedom can separate same-angle users and support target angle-and-distance inference across the surface's two half-spaces.

[[huang-2026-star-ris-nearfield-isac]] jointly optimizes UAV beamforming, surface coefficients, and one horizontal hover location under user-rate and sensing constraints. The result assumes the prescribed near-/far-field partition, available channel and location knowledge, continuous surface control, and a static frame; it is a local simulation solution rather than a robust hardware or mobility validation.
