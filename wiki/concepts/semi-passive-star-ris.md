# Semi-Passive STAR-RIS

A semi-passive STAR-RIS combines passive simultaneous transmission/reflection elements with a smaller set of active sensing elements. The passive aperture controls communication and illumination paths, while local active sensors receive target echoes without forcing the echo through another long surface-to-base-station hop.

[[huang-2026-star-ris-nearfield-isac]] mounts an active sensing array on the transmission side of a large [[star-ris]] for air-ground [[integrated-sensing-and-communication]]. Its optimization omits sensor power, calibration, mutual coupling, processing load, and hardware cost, so reduced sensing path loss does not by itself establish practical energy or cost efficiency.
