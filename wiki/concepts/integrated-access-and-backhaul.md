# Integrated Access and Backhaul (IAB)

A relay architecture in which the same wireless infrastructure carries user-access traffic and upstream backhaul traffic. End-to-end service is limited by the weaker segment, so a strong access link can still fail operationally when the relay's backhaul cannot drain incoming data. In [[yu-2026-ris-uav-iab-outage]], UAVs are mobile IAB nodes and a rooftop [[intelligent-reflecting-surface|RIS]] reinforces their donor-BS backhaul in an obstructed urban environment.
