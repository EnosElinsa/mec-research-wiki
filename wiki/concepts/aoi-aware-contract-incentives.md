# AoI-Aware Contract Incentives

AoI-aware contract incentives combine a freshness metric with principal-agent mechanism design. The principal rewards agents not merely for participation, but for service effort or update frequency that improves [[age-of-information|data freshness]] under hidden private costs or capabilities.

[[guo-2026-aoi-uav-mcs-contract]] applies this idea to uav-assisted-mobile-crowd-sensing. The platform designs one contract layer for UAV service slots and another for user update frequency under sensing/computation costs. Compared with [[stackelberg-game]] or [[reverse-auction-incentive]] baselines, the contract framing is specifically meant for incomplete information: UAVs and users self-select contract items that reveal their types while satisfying individual rationality and incentive compatibility.
