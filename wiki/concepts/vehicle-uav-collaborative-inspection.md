# Vehicle-UAV Collaborative Inspection

Vehicle-UAV collaborative inspection uses ground vehicles as mobile carriers, launch/recovery platforms, and coordination points for aerial infrastructure inspection. The routing problem couples road accessibility and vehicle timing with UAV endurance, aerial task assignment, and launch/recovery synchronization.

[[zhang-2026-msialns-air-ground-inspection]] permits multi-task UAV sorties and cross-vehicle recovery while locking occupied ground nodes. Other corpus entries emphasize complementary concerns: [[qi-2026-drone-vehicle-mec-inspection]] couples inspection with edge computation, [[guo-2026-aot-uav-inspection-offloading]] couples trajectory and task offloading, and [[jia-2026-ufsp-rail-inspection]] studies decentralized multi-UAV rail inspection.
