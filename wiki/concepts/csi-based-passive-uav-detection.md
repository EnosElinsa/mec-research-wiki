# CSI-Based Passive UAV Detection

A passive sensing pattern that detects an uncooperative UAV from temporal changes it induces in an ambient radio channel. In [[bai-2026-passive-uav-detection]], a ground receiver synchronizes to DTMB frame headers, estimates channel impulse responses, and applies multi-scale and multi-period temporal feature extraction to distinguish no-UAV, hovering, vertical flight, and two horizontal orientations without a dedicated transmitter or UAV payload.
