# Two-Regime Aerial-User Association

An altitude-dependent association pattern in a terrestrial/aerial network. At lower altitude, terrestrial blockage and improving LoS probability shape tier preference; closer to the aerial-site altitude, geometric proximity can make the aerial tier dominant.

[[shi-2026-vhetnet-comp-coverage]] derives a scenario-dependent critical height and conditionally analyzes a U-shaped aerial-tier association probability. The reported approximately 110 m boundary belongs to the evaluated parameters, and the two-root result assumes the association function is continuous, strictly U-shaped, and has a unique minimum.
