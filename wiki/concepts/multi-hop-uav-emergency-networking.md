# Multi-Hop UAV Emergency Networking

Multi-hop UAV emergency networking uses a mobile aerial relay graph to connect users in disaster-created dead zones to surviving terrestrial base stations. A UAV is serviceable through either a direct base-station link or a chain of qualifying UAV links, while users attach through connected UAVs and the swarm adjusts position to preserve backhaul and access coverage.

[[xu-2026-mrlmn-llm-multihop]] learns planar swarm motion for coverage and aggregate rate under SNR-threshold connectivity. Its simulation assumes unobstructed UAV-UAV and base-station-UAV links, fixed per-link-class bandwidth, and globally aggregated network information; debris blockage, interference, contention, energy, replacement continuity, and real deployment are outside the evidence.
