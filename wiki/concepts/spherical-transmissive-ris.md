# Spherical Transmissive RIS

A spherical transmissive RIS (ST-RIS) is a curved RIS architecture intended to reduce the angle-sensitive gain loss of planar transmissive RIS panels. In the base-station design of [[liu-2026-spherical-t-ris-bs]], one omnidirectional antenna feeds a spherical RIS, replacing a conventional antenna array while maintaining more stable gain over 3-D incidence/departure directions.

The corpus already includes RIS as reflecting assistance ([[intelligent-reflecting-surface]]), simultaneous transmission/reflection ([[star-ris]]), active amplification ([[active-ris]]), and UAV-mounted surface control ([[uav-mounted-ris]]). ST-RIS adds a base-station-architecture branch: the surface is not just assisting a link, but becomes the radiating component tested in a low-altitude UAV data-collection scenario.
