# UAV-to-UAV Communication

A direct air-to-air data link between UAVs, used for coordination, payload exchange, relaying, or distributed sensing without routing every packet through terrestrial infrastructure. When integrated with cellular spectrum, U2U links must coexist with ground-user and base-station traffic.

[[azari-2020-uav-to-uav-cellular]] analyzes U2U pairs sharing cellular uplink resources. Its stochastic-geometry model compares concurrent spectrum reuse with orthogonal bandwidth partition and exposes how altitude, UAV density, link distance, antenna patterns, and [[fractional-power-control]] reshape both aerial and terrestrial coverage.
