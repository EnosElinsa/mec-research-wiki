# Redundant Resource Reallocation

A threshold-oriented allocation rule that removes resources beyond what already-qualified users need and assigns them to unqualified users. [[chen-2026-laser-powered-multiuav-qoe]] jointly reclaims UAV transmit power and backhaul capacity so more users cross rate- and delay-derived QoE thresholds, rather than maximizing sum rate alone.
