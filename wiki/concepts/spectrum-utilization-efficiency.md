# Spectrum Utilization Efficiency

Spectrum utilization efficiency (SUE) measures useful information transfer per unit bandwidth and per unit spatial footprint. In 3-D aerial networks, the spatial footprint is volumetric, so SUE complements link-level spectral efficiency by asking how efficiently a frequency resource is reused across space and time under interference and partial channel loading.

[[prabhath-not-in-parse-3d-space-spectrum-utilization]] uses SUE for UAV-enabled cellular networks, where [[three-dimensional-frequency-reuse]] controls co-channel interferer geometry and the link model spans free-space, log-normal, and Nakagami-m fading. The metric is adjacent to [[temporal-spectrum-cartography]], which estimates spectrum occupancy from sparse sensing, and [[overlay-underlay-spectrum-access]], which constrains spectrum sharing in cognitive-radio offloading.
