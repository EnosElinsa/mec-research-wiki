# Laser Power Transfer

Directed optical wireless energy delivery from a ground laser station to an optical receiver or collector on an aerial platform, followed by energy conversion. [[chen-2026-laser-powered-multiuav-qoe]] associates each laser station with one UAV access point and couples harvested power to UAV placement, user association, communication power, and QoE. Its narrow beam can deliver high power but makes alignment, blockage, weather, and safety central deployment concerns.
