# Angle-Dependent IRS Effective Aperture

An orientation-sensitive reflection model in which surface gain falls with both incidence and departure angle and vanishes as either path approaches the surface plane. Feasible geometry must also keep the transmitter and served receivers in the reflecting half-space.

[[liu-2026-passive-6dma]] uses a cosine-product aperture term while jointly choosing UAV-mounted-IRS position and orientation. This is related to [[tilt-aware-aerial-ris-control]] but is not a general fading law or a hardware-validated surface model.
