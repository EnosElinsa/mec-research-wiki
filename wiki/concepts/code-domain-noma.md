# Code-Domain NOMA

A non-orthogonal multiple-access family in which simultaneous users are separated by sparse or low-correlation spreading signatures and recovered with multi-user detection or successive interference cancellation. This differs from power-domain [[noma|NOMA]], where users share a resource block at deliberately different power levels. [[krishna-m-2026-multiuav-nbiot]] assigns Zadoff-Chu codes to simultaneous NB-IoT devices and applies estimated-channel SIC, with two users per group under imperfect cancellation.
