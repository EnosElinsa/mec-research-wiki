# Outage-Aware SAGIN UAV Altitude

Altitude selection for a ground-UAV-satellite relay subject to both communication and power-supply failure. [[tan-2025-sagin-outage-altitude]] derives energy-outage and SNR-outage probabilities under solar harvesting plus backup battery, then chooses an altitude that balances ground-air and air-satellite rates while satisfying the modeled outage conditions. Its additional GAS-versus-direct capacity comparison is a link-mode transmission-gap criterion, not multi-user fairness.
