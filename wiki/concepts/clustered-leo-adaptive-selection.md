# Clustered LEO Adaptive Selection

Clustered LEO adaptive selection is a communication-layer strategy in which a terrestrial user chooses between direct satellite transmission and a UAV-assisted cooperative path from a satellite cluster. The selection rule tries to capture spatial diversity without requiring full coherent combining across the cluster.

In [[yang-2026-clustered-leo-adaptive-selection]], the concept is analyzed with spherical stochastic geometry, a 3-D to 2-D density transformation, shadowed-Rician satellite channels, Gamma approximations for aggregate interference/noise, and conditional coverage-probability bounds. It is adjacent to [[leo-satellite-edge-computing]] because LEO-edge offloading models often rely on the availability of this kind of satellite/UAV access layer.
