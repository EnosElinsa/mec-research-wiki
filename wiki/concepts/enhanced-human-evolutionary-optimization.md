# Enhanced Human Evolutionary Optimization

An evolutionary search variant that augments human evolutionary optimization with salp-swarm follower movement and the whale optimizer's shrinking-encircling/spiral operators. [[liu-2026-heterogeneous-sensor-satisfaction]] uses mixed real/binary individuals for UAV locations and sensor associations, penalties for constraint violations, and a separate Lagrange-dual power update inside candidate evaluation.

The operators broaden exploration and exploitation empirically, but they do not turn the mixed non-convex problem into an exact method. Claims should therefore be tied to the paper's repeated simulation comparisons and exhaustive small-instance check rather than treated as a global convergence guarantee.
