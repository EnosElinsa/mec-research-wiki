# Quantum MARL for SAGIN Access

A multi-agent scheduling representation in which parameterized quantum circuits encode network state and basis measurements produce probabilities over combinatorial access actions. [[kim-2026-qmarl-sagin-access]] maps each CubeSat/HALE-UAV decision to one qubit, trains ground-station actors with a centralized critic, and optimizes differentiated access utility plus residual energy in an ideal circuit simulator.
