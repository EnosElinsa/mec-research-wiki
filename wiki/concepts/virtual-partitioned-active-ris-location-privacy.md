# Virtual-Partitioned Active-RIS Location Privacy

A surface-control architecture that physically separates communication gain from adversarial localization interference. [[chen-2026-aris-location-privacy]] assigns one active-RIS partition to legitimate UAV links and separate artificial-noise partitions to malicious RSS localizers, then allocates elements, power, beamforming, and reflection phases to trade legitimate sum rate against localization RMSE.
