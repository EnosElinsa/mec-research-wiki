# UAV-Bus-Taxi Emergency Response

UAV-bus-taxi emergency response uses scheduled buses as mobile charging/hosting platforms and recruits unoccupied taxis as optional outbound or return relays. The UAV can spend less battery reaching and rejoining its bus, leaving more energy for hovering over an incident.

[[xia-2026-ubt-emergency-response]] models four direct/taxi-assisted modes and scores each by response delay, hovering duration, reserve energy, and taxi cost. Historical trajectories predict free-taxi availability and travel time before buses carrying UAVs are selected for citywide spatiotemporal coverage.
