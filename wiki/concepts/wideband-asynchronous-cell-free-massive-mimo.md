# Wideband Asynchronous Cell-Free Massive MIMO

A cell-free downlink in which geographically distributed AP signals reach users with propagation and multipath delays large enough to exceed the cyclic prefix. Delay-induced phase rotation, ICI, ISI, beam split, AP power limits, and distributed hardware resources must then be handled jointly.

[[hong-2026-beam-delay-alignment]] studies ground millimeter-wave APs serving UAV users, reversing the aerial-AP/ground-user roles in [[aerial-terrestrial-cell-free-massive-mimo]]. It assumes path angles, delays, and large-scale statistics are known and does not validate synchronization hardware over the air.
