# Spatially Separated UAV ISAC Role Scheduling

Per-slot assignment of cooperative mobile ISAC nodes to sensing-transmitter and echo-receiver roles while both groups may continue serving communication users. The separation avoids direct full-duplex self-interference and makes role decisions part of the sensing geometry rather than fixing each platform's function.

[[wang-2026-robust-anti-uav-isac]] couples this binary role schedule to user association, transceiver beamforming, 3-D trajectory control, and worst-case cooperative position-CRB minimization. It extends [[cooperative-isac-transceiver-beamforming]] from coordinated beams to coordinated mobile roles.
