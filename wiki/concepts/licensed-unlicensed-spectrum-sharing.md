# Licensed and Unlicensed Spectrum Sharing

Licensed and unlicensed spectrum sharing lets a secondary aerial network combine protected cellular resources with opportunistic Wi-Fi-band capacity. The controller must allocate channels and power while respecting incumbent interference limits in both domains; open unlicensed access also broadens the attack surface for jamming.

[[ding-2026-optimization-driven-spectrum-sharing]] assigns secondary users across licensed primary-user subchannels and unlicensed Wi-Fi subchannels, adding unlicensed resources when licensed service is insufficient. It jointly controls assignments, powers, and UAV position under uncertain jammer CSI.
