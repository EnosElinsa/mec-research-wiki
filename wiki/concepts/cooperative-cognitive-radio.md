# Cooperative Cognitive Radio

A secondary network earns spectrum access by helping a primary transmission, commonly through relaying, while respecting the primary receiver's quality-of-service requirement. The cooperation couples the two networks: secondary power or scheduling cannot be optimized independently of primary service.

In [[wang-2026-covert-cognitive-radio]], one UAV decode-and-forward relay superposes forwarded primary data and a covert secondary signal. The useful primary waveform also becomes [[primary-signal-assisted-covertness|masking interference]] at wardens, so cooperation, primary QoS, and covertness are optimized together.
