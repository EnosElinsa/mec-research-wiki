# Gale-Shapley Rematching

A post-processing refinement that revisits poorly weighted pairs after a stable Gale-Shapley assignment. [[chen-2026-laser-powered-multiuav-qoe]] uses it for user-to-access-point and laser-station-to-UAV association, accepting rematches that improve the communication/harvested-energy objective beyond the initial preference-stable result.
