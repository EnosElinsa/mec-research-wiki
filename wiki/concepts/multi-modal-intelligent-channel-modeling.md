# Multi-Modal Intelligent Channel Modeling

Multi-modal intelligent channel modeling uses non-RF sensing data alongside RF channel data to parameterize propagation. In [[bai-2026-multimodal-uav-vehicle-channel]], LiDAR point clouds help classify static, terrestrial-dynamic, and aerial-dynamic scatterers for multi-UAV-to-multi-vehicle links.

The concept is adjacent to [[integrated-sensing-and-communication]], but it is not just radar sensing over a communication waveform. Its channel-modeling role is to map physical-environment observations into electromagnetic-space parameters so that time, space, and frequency non-stationarity can be modeled more explicitly.
