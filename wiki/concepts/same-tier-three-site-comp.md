# Same-Tier Three-Site CoMP

A CoMP restriction in which exactly three serving sites come from one infrastructure tier: either three aerial base stations or three terrestrial base stations. Cross-tier sites still interfere, but mixed aerial-terrestrial serving triads are excluded.

[[shi-2026-vhetnet-comp-coverage]] adopts this restriction after mixed triads occur infrequently in one simulated configuration. That observation motivates the model but does not prove mixed-tier cooperation is negligible in other deployments.
