# Wireless Information Surveillance

A legitimate monitor attempts to decode a suspicious wireless transmission for authorized surveillance. Passive designs improve the monitoring channel; proactive designs may also relay, jam, or otherwise influence the suspicious link.

[[lin-2026-fc-ris-surveillance]] uses a UAV-borne [[fully-connected-ris]] and receive-antenna selection without an active jamming signal. [[guo-2024-multiuav-proactive-eavesdropping]] instead coordinates monitoring UAVs with proactive interference and relaying, illustrating a distinct intervention regime.

This authorized decoding task is distinct from camera-based service in [[zhan-2026-star-ris-aerial-monitoring]] and motion-state continuity in [[uav-trajectory-monitoring]]. It also inverts the observation outcome of [[primary-signal-assisted-covertness]] and [[full-duplex-receiver-jamming]], which reduce an unauthorized warden's activity evidence. See [[aerial-observation-control-covertness-surveillance-and-monitoring]].
