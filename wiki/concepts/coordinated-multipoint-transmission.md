# Coordinated Multi-Point Transmission

CoMP coordinates multiple geographically separated transmission sites so their desired signals jointly serve a user while interference is managed across sites. Performance depends on synchronization, channel knowledge, cluster selection, backhaul latency, and whether cooperation is coherent or noncoherent.

[[shi-2026-vhetnet-comp-coverage]] analyzes coherent same-tier three-site service for aerial users in a terrestrial/aerial heterogeneous network. [[jin-2026-jitter-aware-uav-comp]] instead studies CSI aging and prediction for distributed multi-UAV CoMP.
