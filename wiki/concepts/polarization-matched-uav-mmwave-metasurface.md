# Polarization-Matched UAV mmWave Metasurface

A transmissive two-layer radio front end that treats polarization mismatch separately from beam direction. In [[zhang-2026-polarfix-uav-mmwave]], a passive linear-to-circular layer reduces received-power sensitivity to rotation of a linear UAV antenna, while a position-controlled 1-bit phase layer restores gain and steers one or more beams. Fabry-Perot spacing aligns repeated transmissions constructively, and the module leaves COTS 802.11ad transceivers unchanged.
