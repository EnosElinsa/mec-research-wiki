# UAV-Energy-Supplied STAR-RIS NOMA

UAV-energy-supplied STAR-RIS NOMA uses an aerial RF-energy source to charge ground users and a fixed simultaneous-transmit-and-reflect surface before uplink power-domain NOMA transmission. The design couples harvest/transmit time, user powers, and transmission/reflection coefficients under energy-causality and rate constraints.

[[meng-2026-star-ris-uav-energy]] solves these blocks through an alternating SDR/SCA, fractional-transform, and game-theoretic time-allocation loop. The UAV follows a predetermined fixed-altitude route and its flight energy is not optimized, while the main communication formulation assumes full CSI.
