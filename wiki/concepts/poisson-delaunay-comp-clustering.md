# Poisson-Delaunay CoMP Clustering

A stochastic-geometry cluster rule that uses Delaunay triangles over network sites to define tractable three-site cooperation groups. It avoids exhaustive strongest-site ranking and connects point-process distance laws to coordinated-coverage analysis.

[[shi-2026-vhetnet-comp-coverage]] applies the rule separately to finite aerial-site and Poisson terrestrial-site tiers. The resulting coverage expressions remain conditional on the BPP/PPP geometry, same-tier serving restriction, fading assumptions, and Gamma approximations used by the paper.
