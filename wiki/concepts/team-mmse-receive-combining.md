# Team-MMSE Receive Combining

Distributed receive combining formulated as a quadratic team-decision problem. Each node uses local instantaneous CSI plus statistical information about other nodes, and coupled stationary conditions yield a block linear system for the combiners.

In [[wang-2026-6dara-cellfree]], the solution is team-optimal and unique for fixed association and aerial geometry under the stated information structure. That scoped guarantee does not extend to the paper's association, position, rotation, or learned control layers.
