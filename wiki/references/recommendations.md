## Rationale — how these map to the current wiki

Read against `wiki/overview.md`'s track table, the corpus is **deep on UAV-MEC + DRL (6 src) and the CMOP/evolutionary lineage (6 src)** but thin elsewhere. The picks above target both axes.

**Depth (shore up the foundations).** Four references are cited by 5+ of the 39 curated papers yet are not themselves curated — they are the corpus's load-bearing works:

- Yang, Bi & Zhang, "Online trajectory and resource optimization for stochastic UAV-enabled MEC" (TWC 2022) — **cited 8×**, the single most central uncurated work. It anchors the trajectory+offloading co-design that the UAV-MEC + DRL and joint-trajectory tracks are built on.
- Zhao et al., "Multi-agent DRL for task offloading in UAV-assisted MEC" (TWC 2022) — **cited 7×**; a canonical MADRL-offloading reference behind the CTDE pattern noted in the overview's cross-cutting observation #2.
- Chen et al., "Energy efficient task offloading … air-ground integrated MEC" (TMC 2023) — **cited 7×**; doubles as a breadth pick (see below).
- Yu et al., "Joint task offloading and resource allocation in UAV-enabled MEC" (IoTJ 2020) — **cited 6×**; foundational problem-formulation reference.

Curating these turns implicit shared ancestry into explicit wiki nodes and would give the `drl-backbones-across-uav-mec-sources` and `cmop-evolutionary-uav-mec-lineage` synthesis pages real citation scaffolding.

**Breadth (open/grow thin tracks).** The overview flags several tracks at 1–2 sources. Strong, recent, in-scope candidates exist for each:

- **Energy efficiency & WPT (2 src)** — Chen et al. (TMC 2023, cited 7×) and the SWIPT-MEC DRL paper (IoTJ 2025) both land here; the first is the rare breadth+depth pick.
- **Maritime MEC (2 src)** — a whole cluster of 2025 TVT/IoTJ maritime-offloading papers (Stackelberg–bargaining, three-tier, AAV+vessel cooperation, space-air-marine) is available to triple this track.
- **Generative-AI MEC (2 src)** — "Distributed foundation models for multi-modal learning in 6G" (IEEE Wireless Commun. 2024, cited 3×) plus a COMST 2025 survey and a TMC 2025 diffusion-DRL paper would move this from placeholder to a real track.
- **ISAC (2 src)** and **Anti-jamming / security-DRL (1 src — the thinnest track)** — recent UAV-ISAC and secure-ISAC/anti-eavesdropping papers (IEEE Wireless Commun. / TCOM 2024-25) are the natural next reads.

## Method notes & caveats

- **Provenance:** `cited_count` counts **distinct curated papers** (the 41 raw folders collapse to 39; two papers were parsed twice). It reflects citation frequency *within this corpus only*, not global citation counts.
- **Correctness:** every field is extracted from the parsed reference string. Fields absent from a string are left `n/a`. Venue tier was assigned from the allow-list in the agent spec; abbreviation expansions (e.g. TWC, TMC, IoTJ) are venue-name normalizations, not new data.
- **Recency skew:** the corpus is heavy on UAV-MEC, so the most-cited references skew toward UAV trajectory/offloading classics (2018-2024). Year is a ranking signal, not a filter — older works surface only when highly central.
- **`in raw/ already? = no`** for all rows: these candidates' own PDFs are not yet parsed under `raw/sources/`, so each needs fetching + parsing before it can be curated.
