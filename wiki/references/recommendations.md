## Method notes & caveats

- **Provenance:** `cited_count` counts **distinct curated papers** (the 84 raw folders collapse to 82; 2 papers were parsed twice). It reflects citation frequency *within this corpus only*, not global citation counts.
- **Correctness:** every field is extracted from the parsed reference string. Fields absent from a string are left `n/a`. Venue tier was assigned from the allow-list in the agent spec; abbreviation expansions (e.g. TWC, TMC, IoTJ) are venue-name normalizations, not new data.
- **Unquoted reference styles:** papers whose bibliographies omit quotation marks (e.g. the DRUDM-CFG parse, `Authors. Title. Venue, vol(no):pp, year.`) are parsed via a sentence-boundary fallback, so their references contribute titles/authors and merge with quoted copies elsewhere in the corpus. OCR-garbled headings (e.g. `# Ref erences`) are also handled. Standards (IEEE/3GPP) and textbooks that genuinely have no `Authors. Title.` shape remain title-less by design and are left `n/a` rather than guessed.
- **Venue-tier across variants:** a work's tier is taken as the strongest tier across *all* its observed citation strings, so a reference is not mis-tiered just because its first-seen copy spelled the venue out in full.
- **Recency skew:** the corpus is heavy on UAV-MEC, so the most-cited references skew toward UAV trajectory/offloading classics (2018-2024). Year is a ranking signal, not a filter — older works surface only when highly central.
- **`in raw/ already? = no`** for all rows: these candidates' own PDFs are not yet parsed under `raw/sources/`, so each needs fetching + parsing before it can be curated.
