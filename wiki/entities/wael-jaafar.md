# Wael Jaafar

Wireless-networks researcher. The 2021 byline places him with the Department of Systems and Computer Engineering, Carleton University; the 2024 byline places him with the Department of Software and IT Engineering, École de Technologie Supérieure (ÉTS), Montreal. The unchanged coauthor group and topic across both papers support treating this as one researcher who moved institutions.

- [[cherif-2021-disconnectivity-aware-cargo-uav]] - dynamic-programming cargo-UAV trajectory with handoff and disconnectivity limits.
- [[cherif-2024-rl-cargo-uav-cell-association]] - Q-learning joint cargo-UAV trajectory and cell association.
