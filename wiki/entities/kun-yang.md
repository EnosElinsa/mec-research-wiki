# Kun Yang

IEEE Fellow and Chair Professor at Nanjing University, with affiliated roles at the University of Essex and University of Electronic Science and Technology of China. Matching biographies report a UCL Ph.D. and research in wireless networks, communication-computing cooperation, and AI for wireless; the STAR-RIS source lists `kyang@ieee.org`.

Co-authored 4 sources in this wiki:

- [[hu-2019-uav-relay-edge-computing]] - joint UAV relaying, MEC scheduling, resource allocation, and trajectory control.
- [[hu-2026-latency-hybrid-uav-mec]] - latency-aware TDMA/NOMA offloading with 3-D UAV trajectory and charging.
- [[xiao-2025-star-ris-bidirectional-uav-mec]] - STAR-RIS-assisted bidirectional task offloading to ground and UAV MEC servers.
- [[wang-2025-cellular-uav-cooperative-detection]] - cellular-connected UAV cooperative ISAC detection and data fusion.
