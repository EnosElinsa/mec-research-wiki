# Dong-Jun Han

IEEE Member. Affiliated with the **Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Purdue University** (West Lafayette, IN, USA); email `han762@purdue.edu`. First author of both sources below.

Co-authored 2 sources in this wiki:

- [[han-2024-ground-satellite-fl]] — cooperative federated learning over ground-to-satellite integrated networks; joint local computation and data offloading with a convergence guarantee.
- [[han-2024-sagin-fl-handover]] — orchestrating federated learning across space-air-ground integrated networks; adaptive inter-layer data offloading + seamless satellite handover.

## Contributions to this wiki

Lead author of the corpus's two non-terrestrial **federated-learning** offloading papers, both from the Purdue group with [[christopher-brinton]] (and Mung Chiang / David J. Love / Seyyedali Hosseinalipour). The two papers are distinct: a two-tier ground-to-satellite setting with solar-battery-aware offloading versus a three-tier SAGIN setting adding a UAV/air layer and seamless handover.
