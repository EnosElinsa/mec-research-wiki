# Akram Al-Hourani

Researcher in air-to-ground and non-terrestrial channel modeling. The 2014 byline lists the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, RMIT University; the single-author 2020 and 2024 letters give no affiliation in the parse. The distinctive full name and the continuous line of stochastic-geometry LoS modeling across all three papers support one identity.

- [[al-hourani-2014-optimal-lap-altitude]] - optimal low-altitude platform altitude for maximum coverage.
- [[al-hourani-2020-urban-los-probability]] - stochastic-geometry LoS probability between arbitrary heights in urban areas.
- [[al-hourani-2024-los-holding-distance-ntn]] - LoS probability and holding distance with a continuous-space Markov chain.
