# Mohammadsaleh Nikooroo

Researcher on flying base stations. Both biographies report a 2014 B.Sc. from Sharif University of Technology and a 2017 M.Phil. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, followed by Ph.D. studies, and both papers are coauthored with Zdenek Becvar, which establishes one identity.

- [[becvar-2022-airship-flybs-energy]] - flying energy of airship-based flying base stations serving moving users.
- [[nikooroo-2022-flybs-total-power]] - total propulsion and transmission power of a flying base station.
