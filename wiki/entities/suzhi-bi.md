# Suzhi Bi

IEEE Senior Member and professor with the **College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Shenzhen University**. The GeoAgg-HSAC biography and the current sparse-XL-MIMO PDF repeat his Zhejiang University B.E., Chinese University of Hong Kong Ph.D., NUS postdoctoral work, and Shenzhen University affiliation, establishing a single identity.

Co-authored three sources in this wiki:

- [[min-2026-sparse-bistatic-nearfield-isac]] - sparse XL-MIMO and fourth-order virtual arrays for low-altitude UAV-swarm sensing.
- [[yang-2022-stochastic-uav-mec-lyapunov]] - Lyapunov online trajectory and resource optimization for stochastic UAV-MEC.
- [[xie-2026-geoagg-hsac]] - terrain-aware graph aggregation and hybrid-action SAC for mountainous localization/communication.

## Contributions to this wiki

- Contributes wireless optimization and learning across stochastic UAV-MEC, terrain-aware UAV localization, and near-field physical-layer ISAC.
