# Hao Hao

First author of two sources in this wiki, both on **priority-aware computation offloading** in MEC, with an identical co-author roster (Changqiao Xu, Wei Zhang, Shujie Yang, Gabriel-Miro Muntean):

- [[hao-2024-clp-multiuav-priority-offloading]] — multi-UAV cooperative MEC with task priority; CLP ([[td3]] + [[hybrid-action-representation]]).
- [[hao-2025-priority-aware-task-driven-co]] — task-driven (event-driven) priority-aware offloading via DRL.

## Contributions to this wiki

- Anchors the corpus's **task-priority** thread ([[task-priority-in-mec]], [[priority-based-delay-utility]]): both papers replace preemptive scheduling with asymmetric priority utilities so high-priority tasks meet deadlines without starving low-priority ones.
- Recurring use of **hybrid (discrete-continuous) action** DRL ([[hybrid-action-decision-making]]) — a learned latent action representation in the 2024 paper and a dependence-aware latent space in the 2025 paper.

(The two works are closely related: the 2025 task-driven paper reads as a follow-on to the 2024 slot-driven multi-UAV formulation.)
