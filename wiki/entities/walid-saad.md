# Walid Saad

IEEE Senior Member (on these works). Affiliated with **Wireless@VT, Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, Virginia Tech** (Blacksburg/VA, USA; email `walids@vt.edu`).

Co-authored 8 sources in this wiki, all foundational UAV-communications works:

- [[mozaffari-2016-uav-underlaid-d2d]] — UAV downlink base station coexisting with an underlaid D2D network; stochastic-geometry coverage/rate + altitude / disk-covering tradeoffs.
- [[mozaffari-2017-uav-iot-energy-efficient]] — joint UAV 3D placement, mobility, device association, and uplink power control for energy-efficient IoT data collection.
- [[mozaffari-2019-uav-wireless-tutorial]] — the IEEE COMST tutorial on UAVs for wireless networks.
- [[mozaffari-2019-drone-antenna-array]] — drone-based antenna array steered by repositioning the drones; minimum-service-time communications and control.
- [[mozaffari-not-in-parse-3d-drone-cellular-network]] - 3-D aerial cellular network with LAP drone-BSs, cellular-connected drone-UEs, HAP/FSO backhaul, truncated-octahedron frequency reuse, and optimal-transport association. *(Parsed metadata lacks DOI/venue/year.)*
- [[mozaffari-2015-drone-small-cells]] - optimal altitude and separation for one- and two-drone small-cell coverage.
- [[mozaffari-2016-efficient-multi-uav-coverage]] - coverage-probability analysis and circle-packing deployment for multiple UAVs.
- [[challita-2019-cellular-uav-interference-drl]] - deep echo-state-network control of cellular-UAV paths, powers, and associations.
