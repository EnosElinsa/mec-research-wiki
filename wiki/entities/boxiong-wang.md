# Boxiong Wang

Affiliated with the **College of Computer Science and Technology, Jilin University** (Changchun, China). Per the parses, he received B.S. and M.S. degrees in software engineering from Jilin University (2021, 2024) and is pursuing a Ph.D. in computer science there; research interests in UAV networks, mobile edge computing, and optimization. Identified consistently by email `wangbx0320@163.com` across his sources.

Co-authored 2 sources in this wiki, both in the Jilin-University / NTU aerial-MEC cluster:

- [[wang-2025-sac-tma-mec-dc]] — first author; joint AAV MEC + data collection via SAC-TMA (matching-enabled DRL).
- [[chen-2025-swipt-mec-sac]] — co-author; energy-efficient UAV-assisted SWIPT-MEC via improved SAC.
