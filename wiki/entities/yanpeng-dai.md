The UAV-ISAC PDF lists `yanpengdai@dlmu.edu.cn` and identifies him as corresponding author; the same exact name and coauthor cluster recur in both graph-resource-management surveys.

- [[dai-2024-graph-rm-survey-optimization]] - graph-optimization methods for wireless resource management.
