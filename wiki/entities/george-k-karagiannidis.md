# George K. Karagiannidis

IEEE Fellow and Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, where the source biography identifies him as head of the Wireless Communications and Information Processing Group. His stated interests include wireless systems and networks, signal processing, optical wireless communication, wireless power transfer, and biomedical signal processing.
