# Nishant Gupta

Researcher in UAV communications. Both biographies report a B.Tech. from Chandigarh Engineering College, Mohali (2015) and an M.Tech. from Panjab University, Chandigarh (2018), and both papers share the coauthors Deepak Mishra and Satyam Agarwal, which establishes one identity.

- [[gupta-2022-energy-aware-outage-trajectory]] - energy-constrained 3-D UAV trajectory for average outage minimization.
- [[gupta-not-in-parse-uav-replacement]] - trajectory and resource allocation for UAV replacement with uninterrupted service.
