# Jingjing Wang

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with the **School of Cyber Science and Technology, Beihang University** (Beijing, China) and the **Hangzhou Innovation Institute, Beihang University** (email `drwangjj@buaa.edu.cn`); earlier work places him at the **Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University** as a Shuimu Tsinghua Scholar. The shared research line (AI/ML-aided next-generation wireless networks, swarm-intelligence ad hoc networks), the Tsinghua-PhD-to-Beihang-faculty career move, and the recurring co-author [[chunxiao-jiang]] confirm a single identity across the papers despite the common name.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[yang-2020-loadbalance-multiuav-iot]] — differential-evolution multi-UAV deployment + DRL task scheduling for load-balanced IoT MEC (Tsinghua-era work, with [[chunxiao-jiang]]).
- [[qin-2025-bcuav-masac]] — cooperative UAV trajectory + resource allocation in blockchain-enabled secure aerial edge computing (MASAC).
- [[wang-2025-acbft-uav-consensus]] — adaptive chained Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus for UAV ad hoc networks (corresponding author).
