# Jingjing Wang

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with the **School of Cyber Science and Technology, Beihang University** (Beijing, China) and the **Hangzhou Innovation Institute, Beihang University** (email `drwangjj@buaa.edu.cn`) — the shared email and Beihang affiliation confirm a single identity across both papers despite the common name.

- [[wang-2025-acbft-uav-consensus]] — adaptive chained Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus for UAV ad hoc networks (corresponding author).
