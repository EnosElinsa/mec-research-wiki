# Hongbin Chen

Professor with the **School of Information and Communication, Guilin University of Electronic Technology** (Guilin, China); email `chbscut@guet.edu.cn` (consistent across all three sources). BEng (Nanjing Univ. of Posts and Telecommunications, 2004) and PhD in circuits and systems (South China University of Technology, 2009), per the author biography in the parses. Research focuses on energy-efficient wireless communications.

Co-authored 4 sources in this wiki, all in the Guilin air-ground / space-air-ground integrated networks line:

- [[li-2024-twohop-iort-packet-scheduling]] — two-hop packet scheduling + resource allocation + UAV trajectory (MADDPG-APER) for IoRT.
- [[li-2025-twohop-airground-drl-offloading]] — two-hop partial task offloading + resource allocation (JPTORAUTD).
- [[li-2024-airground-vec-offloading]] — joint computation offloading + multidimensional resource allocation in an air-ground integrated VEC network (HAP + UAVs + RSU; JCESRA).
- [[wang-2024-hybrid-oma-noma-sagin]] — hybrid OMA/NOMA mode selection + resource allocation in SAGIN (SCA + Lagrange + DQN).
