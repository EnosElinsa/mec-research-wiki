# Haichao Wang

Researcher in UAV communications, interference mitigation, green communications, and convex optimization at the College of Communications Engineering, Army Engineering University of PLA. The 2018 biography reports pursuit of a Ph.D. after the 2014 electronic-engineering B.S.; the two 2026 biographies report completion of that Ph.D. in 2019 and the same institutional affiliation. This consistent progression establishes the identity.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[wang-2018-uav-powered-d2d]] - RF-energy scheduling and power allocation for cochannel D2D pairs supplied by a UAV.
- [[li-2026-noma-uav-relay-planning]] - beamforming, power, and trajectory optimization for NOMA UAV relaying.
- [[du-2026-hierarchical-coalition-deployment]] - hierarchical UAV-deployment and ground-user coalition formation.
