# Jiajia Liu

IEEE Senior Member / Fellow. Affiliated with **Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU)** — National Engineering Laboratory for Integrated Aero-Space-Ground-Ocean Big Data Application Technology and the School of Cybersecurity (Xi'an, China; email `liujiajia@nwpu.edu.cn`) — consistent across his papers in this wiki, frequently a corresponding author.

Co-authored 5 sources in this wiki:

- [[guo-2023-mccco-multiuav-5g-offloading]] — multi-UAV cooperative task offloading and resource allocation in 5G-advanced (corresponding author).
- [[mao-2024-ntn-hierarchical-caching-cav]] — hierarchical content caching for NTN-assisted connected automated vehicles.
- [[mao-2025-bcsa-frl]] — blockchain-enabled cold-start aggregation for FRL-based task offloading in zero-trust LEO satellite networks.
- [[mao-2024-fso-leo-hierarchical-routing]] — hierarchical multi-objective DRL routing for ultra-dense FSO LEO satellite networks (corresponding author).
- [[mao-2025-irs-noma-fl-secrecy]] — IRS-assisted secrecy-rate maximization for NOMA-based federated learning toward 6G.
