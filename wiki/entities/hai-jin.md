# Hai Jin
Chair professor of computer science and engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), with the Services Computing Technology and System Lab and the Cluster and Grid Computing Lab. The EUAGame byline gives hjin@hust.edu.cn at these HUST labs, and the KubeSPT biography gives a 1994 HUST PhD and a HUST chair professorship, which supports one identity.
