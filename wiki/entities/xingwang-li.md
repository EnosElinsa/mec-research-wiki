# Xingwang Li

IEEE Senior Member and Associate Professor in the School of Physics and Electronic Information Engineering at Henan Polytechnic University. Matching biographies report a 2010 M.Sc. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, a 2015 Ph.D. from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, and a 2017-2018 visiting appointment at Queen's University Belfast.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[liu-2026-uav-hsr-jitter]] - jitter-aware outage/rate analysis and adaptive beamwidth for UAV-assisted railway communication.
- [[ahmed-2026-noma-irs-vehicular]] - fixed-point passive-IRS phases and power allocation for NOMA UAV-vehicular links.
- [[mihertie-2026-aerial-irs-rsma-ee]] - RSMA energy-efficiency optimization with an aerial IRS and hardware distortion.
