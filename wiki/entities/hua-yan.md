# Hua Yan

Researcher in UAV-enabled wireless communications and wireless power transfer. The 2020 biography lists a Ph.D. in progress at the University of Warwick; the 2025 biography reports a 2022 Warwick Ph.D., a postdoctoral post at Durham University, and a lecturer role at Leeds Trinity University. Both papers are coauthored with Yunfei Chen, which supports one identity.

- [[yan-2020-uav-wpt-bs-charging]] - UAV charger for ground sensors with base-station loading and UAV power consumption.
- [[yan-2025-in-flight-uav-charging-distance]] - optimum distance for UAV-to-UAV in-flight RF charging and communication.

## Contributions to this wiki

Supplies link-level energy models for [[wireless-power-transfer]] and [[in-flight-uav-energy-replenishment]], from UAV-to-sensor charging to UAV-to-UAV charging in the Fresnel zone.
