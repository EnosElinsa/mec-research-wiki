# Mohammad Mozaffari

Affiliated (in the curated sources) with **Wireless@VT, Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, Virginia Tech** (Blacksburg/VA, USA; email `mmozaff@vt.edu`), where he was a Student Member of IEEE on these works.

Lead author of 4 sources in this wiki, all foundational UAV-communications works:

- [[mozaffari-2016-uav-underlaid-d2d]] — coexistence of a UAV downlink base station with an underlaid D2D network; stochastic-geometry coverage/rate analysis + optimal-altitude and disk-covering mobile-coverage tradeoffs (lead author).
- [[mozaffari-2017-uav-iot-energy-efficient]] — joint 3D placement, mobility, device association, and uplink power control of multiple UAVs for energy-efficient IoT data collection (lead author).
- [[mozaffari-2019-uav-wireless-tutorial]] — the IEEE COMST tutorial on UAVs for wireless networks (UAVs as aerial base stations vs cellular-connected UAVs, 3D deployment, channel modeling, energy efficiency) (lead author).
- [[mozaffari-2019-drone-antenna-array]] — drone-based antenna array that beam-steers by physically repositioning the drones; minimum-service-time design via perturbation-theory drone spacing + bang-bang minimum control time (lead author).
