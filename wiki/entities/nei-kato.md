# Nei Kato

Affiliated with the **Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University** (Sendai, Japan); emails `kato@it.is.tohoku.ac.jp` / `nei.kato.d3@tohoku.ac.jp` across the sources. The biography in [[zhao-2026-heuristic-supervised-drl]] lists him as a Distinguished Professor with the Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University, confirming the same identity.

Co-authored 6 sources in this wiki, spanning the NWPU non-terrestrial-network cluster led by [[bomin-mao]], HSD multi-UAV MEC optimization, and SAGIN outage analysis:

- [[mao-2024-ntn-hierarchical-caching-cav]] - hierarchical content caching for CAVs over NTN (LEO+UAV).
- [[mao-2025-bcsa-frl]] - blockchain-enabled cold-start aggregation for federated RL over zero-trust LEO networks.
- [[mao-2024-fso-leo-hierarchical-routing]] - hierarchical multi-objective DRL routing for ultra-dense FSO LEO satellite networks.
- [[mao-2025-irs-noma-fl-secrecy]] - IRS-assisted secrecy-rate maximization for NOMA-based federated learning toward 6G.
- [[zhao-2026-heuristic-supervised-drl]] - heuristic-supervised DRL framework with TTSSA convergence analysis and a PSO-MARL UAV-MEC case study.
- [[tan-2025-sagin-outage-altitude]] - solar-energy and SNR outage analysis for a ground-UAV-satellite relay.
