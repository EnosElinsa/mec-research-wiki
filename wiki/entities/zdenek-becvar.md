# Zdenek Becvar

Researcher with the Department of Telecommunication Engineering, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Czech Technical University in Prague. The 2017 byline gives this department, and both 2022 biographies report M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees from CTU in 2005 and 2010, which establishes one identity.

- [[mach-2017-mec-survey-architecture]] - survey of MEC architecture and computation offloading.
- [[becvar-2022-airship-flybs-energy]] - flying energy of airship-based flying base stations serving moving users.
- [[nikooroo-2022-flybs-total-power]] - total propulsion and transmission power of a flying base station.
