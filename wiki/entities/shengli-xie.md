# Shengli Xie

Fellow, IEEE. Affiliated with the **Guangdong University of Technology** (Guangzhou, China) — the School of Automation in the CMOP-lineage sources and the Guangdong-HongKong-Macao Joint Laboratory for Smart Discrete Manufacturing in the power-IoT source; email `shlxie@gdut.edu.cn` (consistent across all sources). BS in mathematics (Jilin University, 1983), per the author biography in the parse.

Co-authored 5 sources in this wiki:

- [[huang-2025-cmop-dispersed-computing]] — reliable task offloading in dispersed computing via dual-population CMOEA.
- [[peng-2024-energy-time-uav-its]] — UAV-ITS energy + completion-time-difference CMOP (CMOEA/D-CDP).
- [[liu-2020-cooperative-uav-mec-power-iot]] — cooperative UAV-enabled MEC for power IoT; centralized + distributed DRL over a semi-Markov formulation.
- [[liu-2023-sagecn-online-offloading]] — collaborative space/aerial-aided edge computing for 6G; LEO-as-server-and-user online offloading via Lyapunov optimization + delayed online learning.
- [[qi-2026-drone-vehicle-mec-inspection]] - cooperative drone-vehicle MEC for low-altitude inspection with route planning, battery swapping, and detached-drone speed optimization.
