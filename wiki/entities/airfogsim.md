# AirFogSim

Lightweight modular simulation platform introduced by [[wei-2026-airfogsim-uav-vfc]] for UAV-integrated [[vehicle-fog-computing]]. It models aerial-ground interactive computing networks with traffic, UAV mobility, communication, computation, energy, security/privacy, blockchain, and scheduling components.

Within this wiki, AirFogSim is a tool/entity rather than a method concept: it is useful as experimental infrastructure for testing [[task-offloading]], [[uav-trajectory-control]], and resource-allocation policies in vehicular fog computing settings.
