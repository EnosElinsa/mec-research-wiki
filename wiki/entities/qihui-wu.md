# Qihui Wu

IEEE Fellow. Affiliated with the **College of Electronic and Information Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)**, Nanjing, China (also associated with the Key Laboratory of Dynamic Cognitive System of Electromagnetic Spectrum Space at NUAA) — consistent across his papers in this wiki.

Co-authored 7 sources in this wiki, all in the aerial/UAV-HAP edge-computing and swarm-computing line:

- [[jia-2022-hierarchical-aerial-matching]] — hierarchical aerial computing via cooperation of HAPs and UAVs.
- [[jia-2025-dro-uav-hap-mec]] — distributionally robust optimization for aerial MEC via UAV-HAP cooperation.
- [[liu-2022-miso-uav-mec-trajectory]] — resource allocation and trajectory design for MISO UAV-assisted MEC.
- [[sun-2024-asap-uav-swarm]] — all-sky autonomous collaborative computing in a UAV swarm.
- [[qu-ecoei-uav-swarm]] — eCoEI elastic collaborative edge intelligence architecture for UAV swarms.
- [[you-2025-uncertain-maritime-hasac]] — uncertain maritime MEC via cooperation of AAVs and vessels (HASAC).
- [[jia-2026-dro-lawn-trajectory]] — distributionally robust computation offloading and trajectory optimization in low-altitude wireless networks.
