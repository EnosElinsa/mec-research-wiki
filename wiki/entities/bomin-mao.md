# Bomin Mao

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with **Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU)** — School of Cybersecurity and the National Engineering Laboratory for Integrated Aero-Space-Ground-Ocean Big Data Application Technology (Xi'an / Shenzhen / Taicang, China; email `maobomin@nwpu.edu.cn`) — consistent across his papers in this wiki, where he is the lead author of all four.

Lead author of 4 sources in this wiki, on non-terrestrial-network caching/offloading, LEO routing, and secure federated learning:

- [[mao-2024-ntn-hierarchical-caching-cav]] — hierarchical content caching and asynchronous updating for NTN-assisted connected automated vehicles.
- [[mao-2025-bcsa-frl]] — blockchain-enabled cold-start aggregation for FRL-based task offloading in zero-trust LEO satellite networks.
- [[mao-2024-fso-leo-hierarchical-routing]] — intelligent hierarchical (dual-layer MEO/LEO) multi-objective DRL routing for ultra-dense FSO LEO satellite networks.
- [[mao-2025-irs-noma-fl-secrecy]] — IRS-assisted secrecy-rate maximization for NOMA-based federated-learning model aggregation toward 6G.
