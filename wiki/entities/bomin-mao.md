# Bomin Mao

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with **Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU)** — School of Cybersecurity and the National Engineering Laboratory for Integrated Aero-Space-Ground-Ocean Big Data Application Technology (Xi'an / Shenzhen / Taicang, China; email `maobomin@nwpu.edu.cn`) — consistent across his papers in this wiki, where he is the lead author of both.

Lead author of 2 sources in this wiki, both on non-terrestrial-network caching/offloading:

- [[mao-2024-ntn-hierarchical-caching-cav]] — hierarchical content caching and asynchronous updating for NTN-assisted connected automated vehicles.
- [[mao-2025-bcsa-frl]] — blockchain-enabled cold-start aggregation for FRL-based task offloading in zero-trust LEO satellite networks.

## Contributions to this wiki

- The lead of the NWPU non-terrestrial-network cluster with [[hongzhi-guo]] and [[jiajia-liu]], bringing the LEO-satellite / NTN caching and federated-learning thread to the corpus.
