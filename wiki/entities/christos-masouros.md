# Christos Masouros

IEEE Fellow and Professor at University College London. The 2024 UAV-ISAC parse lists `chris.masouros@ieee.org`, degrees from the University of Patras and University of Manchester, and research in wireless communications, signal processing, green communications, large-scale antennas, ISAC, MIMO interference mitigation, and multicarrier communications.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[su-2024-sensing-aided-isac-pls]] - CRB-guided sensing of eavesdroppers and secrecy-rate optimization.
- [[jing-2024-isac-trajectory-localization]] - multi-stage UAV trajectory design for communication and target localization.
- [[zhang-2026-control-assisted-beam-tracking]] - flight-control-assisted mmWave beam prediction and tracking for UAV links.
