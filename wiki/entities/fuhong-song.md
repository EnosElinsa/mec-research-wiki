# Fuhong Song

Recurring author across evolutionary multi-objective RL and UAV data-collection optimization sources. The affiliation moves between papers — **Southwest Jiaotong University** (Chengdu; PhD-era email `fhs@my.swjtu.edu.cn` in 2022) and **Guizhou University of Finance and Economics** (Guiyang; `fhsong@mail.gufe.edu.cn` in 2024) — but the identity is confirmed by the shared co-author **Huanlai Xing** and the closely related aerial-network optimization research. This is a student-to-faculty move, not a namesake.

Authored sources:

- [[song-2022-emorl-tcto-uav]] — trajectory control + task offloading via evolutionary multi-objective RL (EMORL-TCTO); multi-policy Pareto set in one run.
- [[song-2024-mol-aoi-energy]] — AoI-vs-energy aerial-ground MEC via multi-objective RL (MOL-AET: multi-objective PPO + evolutionary phase).

- [[liu-2026-heterogeneous-sensor-satisfaction]] - heterogeneous-sensor UAV deployment, association, and power allocation through evolutionary search.

## Contributions to this wiki

The connective author between the **evolutionary-RL** and **multi-objective RL** threads — both sources fuse a policy-gradient learner with an evolutionary phase to recover a Pareto set, distinct from the scalarized-reward DRL majority. Relevant to [[evolutionary-reinforcement-learning]] and [[multi-objective-reinforcement-learning]].
