# Qixun Zhang

Professor at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and the Key Laboratory of Universal Wireless Communications, Ministry of Education. Matching biographies report BUPT B.E./B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in 2006 and 2011, visiting appointments at the University of Maryland and University of Houston, and research spanning wireless resource management, mmWave, autonomous-vehicle communication, UAV communication, and ISAC. The 2020 source supplies `zhangqixun@bupt.edu.cn`.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[zhang-2020-response-delay-uav-swarm]] - stochastic-geometry and queueing analysis for response delay in a MEC-enabled UAV swarm.
- [[li-not-in-parse-movable-antenna-pls]] - movable-antenna micro-mobility versus UAV macro-mobility for physical-layer security.
- [[wang-2025-cellular-uav-cooperative-detection]] - BS/UAV cooperative sensing-data fusion and trajectory/beamforming control.
