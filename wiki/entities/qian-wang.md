# Qian Wang

IEEE Member. Associate Professor with the **Institute of Cyberspace Security, Zhejiang University of Technology**, Hangzhou, China. She received the B.Eng. degree in communication engineering from Harbin Engineering University in 2012 and the Ph.D. degree in electrical and computer engineering from the National University of Singapore in 2017. Her stated interests include communication and information theory, physical-layer technologies, signal processing, resource allocation, and network optimization.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki in the marine-IoT energy and security line:

- [[qian-2022-uav-maritime-iot-noma]] - NOMA-based UAV-assisted maritime MEC with DDPG trajectory control and Lagrangian multi-domain resource allocation.
- [[qian-2024-marine-fl-dt-secrecy]] - federated-learning-assisted marine digital twins with NOMA uploads and secrecy-aware HAP broadcasting.
- [[wang-2026-noma-marine-data-computation]] - NOMA sensing-data collection followed by UAV computation, jointly controlled with TD3.

## Contributions to this wiki

Connects three stages of the Zhejiang/Dalian/University-of-Macau maritime collaboration: partial task offloading, secrecy-aware federated marine digital twins, and mandatory sensing-data collection with aerial computation. Across them, NOMA, energy minimization, and wireless resource allocation remain the common thread.
