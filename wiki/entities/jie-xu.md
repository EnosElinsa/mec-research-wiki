# Jie Xu

IEEE Fellow. Affiliated with the **School of Science and Engineering (SSE), The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen** — and, in the secure-ISAC / LAE-ISAC papers, the **Shenzhen Future Network of Intelligence Institute** and the **Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Future Networks of Intelligence**. The four sources all place Jie Xu at CUHK-Shenzhen (SSE / FNii) within the integrated-sensing-and-communication research area, supporting a single identity (a common name, disambiguated by the consistent CUHK-Shenzhen + ISAC affiliation).

Co-author of four ISAC / edge-AI sources in this wiki:

- [[meng-2024-uav-isac-overview]] — overview of UAV-enabled ISAC for 6G (motion control, resource allocation, sensing-communication synergy).
- [[yao-2025-secure-isac-dual-eavesdropping]] — secure UAV-ISAC against dual eavesdropping; joint beamforming + trajectory design.
- [[wen-2024-iscc-edge-ai]] — task-oriented integrated sensing, computation, and communication (ISCC) for multi-device edge-AI inference; discriminant-gain maximization via the sum-of-ratios method.
- [[ye-2026-deeplsc-lae-isac]] — LAE ISAC with DDPG-based joint GBS beamforming and authorized-UAV trajectory control under sensing and flight constraints.
