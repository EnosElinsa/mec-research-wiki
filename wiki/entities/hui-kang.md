# Hui Kang

Professor with the **College of Computer Science and Technology** and the **Key Laboratory of Symbolic Computation and Knowledge Engineering of Ministry of Education, Jilin University** (Changchun, China). Per the parses, she received M.E. and Ph.D. degrees from Jilin University (1996, 2007); research interests in information integration and distributed computing. Identified consistently by email `kanghui@jlu.edu.cn` across her sources.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki, all in the Jilin-University / NTU aerial-MEC cluster:

- [[sun-2021-temcmop-uav-cb]] — UAV collaborative-beamforming time + energy multi-objective optimization (TEMCMOP) via IMOALO (corresponding author).
- [[wang-2025-sac-tma-mec-dc]] — joint AAV MEC + data collection via SAC-TMA.
- [[chen-2025-swipt-mec-sac]] — energy-efficient UAV-assisted SWIPT-MEC via improved SAC.

## Contributions to this wiki

- A faculty co-author in the [[geng-sun]] / Jiahui Li aerial-MEC group, recurring across its UAV-MEC DRL sources. Co-publishes with [[boxiong-wang]], Jiahui Li, Zemin Sun, [[geng-sun]], and the NTU side ([[jiacheng-wang]], [[dusit-niyato]]).
