# Nesrine Cherif

Researcher with the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, University of Ottawa. Both bylines list the same University of Ottawa affiliation and the same coauthors (Wael Jaafar, Halim Yanikomeroglu, and Abbas Yongacoglu), which establishes one identity.

- [[cherif-2021-disconnectivity-aware-cargo-uav]] - dynamic-programming cargo-UAV trajectory with handoff and disconnectivity limits.
- [[cherif-2024-rl-cargo-uav-cell-association]] - Q-learning joint cargo-UAV trajectory and cell association (corresponding author).

## Contributions to this wiki

Defines the cargo-UAV line of [[handoff-aware-uav-trajectory-planning]], moving from heatmap-based dynamic programming to learned cell association.
