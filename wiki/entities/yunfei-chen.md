# Yunfei Chen

Wireless-communications researcher. Every biography reports B.E. and M.E. degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (1998 and 2001) and a Ph.D. from the University of Alberta (2006). The 2018 to 2020 papers list the School of Engineering, University of Warwick (`yunfei.chen@warwick.ac.uk`); the 2025 paper lists the Department of Engineering, Durham University (`yunfei.chen@durham.ac.uk`). The matching degree history supports one researcher who moved institutions.

- [[zhao-2018-caching-uav-ia-secure]] - caching UAV assisted secure transmission with interference alignment.
- [[zhao-2019-uav-emergency-disasters]] - UAV-assisted emergency networks in disasters.
- [[li-2020-maritime-uav-satellite-coverage]] - maritime coverage with UAVs coordinated with hybrid satellite-terrestrial networks.
- [[yan-2020-uav-wpt-bs-charging]] - UAV-enabled WPT with base-station charging and UAV power consumption.
- [[yan-2025-in-flight-uav-charging-distance]] - optimum distance for UAV-to-UAV in-flight wireless charging (corresponding author).
