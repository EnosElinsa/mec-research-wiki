# Chao Dong

IEEE Member / Senior Member. Affiliated with the **College of Electronic and Information Engineering, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)**, Nanjing, China (email `dch@nuaa.edu.cn`) — consistent across his papers in this wiki. Frequently the corresponding author for the NUAA aerial-computing group.

Co-authored 6 sources in this wiki:

- [[jia-2022-hierarchical-aerial-matching]] — hierarchical aerial computing via cooperation of HAPs and UAVs.
- [[jia-2025-dro-uav-hap-mec]] — distributionally robust optimization for aerial MEC via UAV-HAP cooperation.
- [[sun-2024-asap-uav-swarm]] — all-sky autonomous collaborative computing in a UAV swarm.
- [[qu-ecoei-uav-swarm]] — eCoEI elastic collaborative edge intelligence architecture for UAV swarms.
- [[you-2025-uncertain-maritime-hasac]] — uncertain maritime MEC via cooperation of AAVs and vessels (HASAC).
- [[zhai-2026-collaborative-inference-uav-mec]] — DNN partitioning and dynamic trajectory selection for collaborative inference in UAV-assisted MEC.
