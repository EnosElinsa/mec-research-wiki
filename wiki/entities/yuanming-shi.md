# Yuanming Shi

IEEE Senior Member and Professor in the School of Information Science and Technology at ShanghaiTech University. Matching biographies report a 2011 Tsinghua B.S., a 2015 HKUST Ph.D., and continuous ShanghaiTech service since 2015. His stated interests span wireless communications, artificial intelligence, convex optimization, federated learning, edge AI, and satellite networks.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki:

- [[qian-2026-federated-bandit-aircomp]] - UAV-aided AirComp for event-triggered federated linear bandit learning.
- [[wen-2024-iscc-edge-ai]] - task-oriented sensing, computation, and communication integration for multi-device edge inference.
- [[wen-2026-uav-edge-inference-iscc]] - UAV-assisted feature collection and end-to-end inference-delay optimization.
