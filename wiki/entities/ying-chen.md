# Ying Chen

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with **Beijing Information Science and Technology University** (Beijing, China) — email `chenying@bistu.edu.cn`, consistent across both curated sources, confirming a single identity (the name is common, but the shared email + institution + recurring co-authors Yuan Wu and Jiwei Huang disambiguate it).

First author of two sources in this wiki:

- [[chen-2023-dotora-air-ground-online]] — energy-efficient task offloading + resource allocation in air-ground integrated MEC; distributed online approach (DGMS/TPA/DOTORA).
- [[chen-2024-ulse-game]] — multi-user task offloading in UAV-assisted LEO satellite edge computing as a potential game (LUTO-Game / JULTO).

## Contributions to this wiki

Anchors the air-ground / UAV-LEO **online and game-theoretic offloading** thread. Both works share co-authors [[yuan-wu]] (University of Macau) and Jiwei Huang (China University of Petroleum), and span the distributed-online ([[chen-2023-dotora-air-ground-online]]) and potential-game ([[chen-2024-ulse-game]]) formulations — the latter feeding the [[game-theoretic-offloading-formulations]] comparison.
