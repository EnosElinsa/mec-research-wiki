# Ying Chen

IEEE Senior Member. Affiliated with **Beijing Information Science and Technology University** (Beijing, China) — email `chenying@bistu.edu.cn`, consistent across her curated sources, confirming a single identity (the name is common, but the shared email + institution + recurring co-authors Jie Zhao, Yuan Wu, and Jiwei Huang disambiguate it).

First author of three sources in this wiki:

- [[chen-2023-dotora-air-ground-online]] — energy-efficient task offloading + resource allocation in air-ground integrated MEC; distributed online approach (DGMS/TPA/DOTORA).
- [[chen-2024-ulse-game]] — multi-user task offloading in UAV-assisted LEO satellite edge computing as a potential game (LUTO-Game / JULTO).
- [[chen-2022-qoe-game-end-edge-cloud]] — QoE-aware decentralized task offloading for end-edge-cloud systems as a potential game (MUTO-Game / GDTO; with Price-of-Anarchy analysis).

## Contributions to this wiki

Anchors the air-ground / UAV-LEO / end-edge-cloud **online and game-theoretic offloading** thread. The works share co-authors [[yuan-wu]] (University of Macau), Jiwei Huang (China University of Petroleum), and (on the two game-theoretic papers) [[xuemin-shen]] (Waterloo), and span the distributed-online ([[chen-2023-dotora-air-ground-online]]) and potential-game ([[chen-2024-ulse-game]], [[chen-2022-qoe-game-end-edge-cloud]]) formulations — the latter two feeding the [[game-theoretic-offloading-formulations]] comparison.
