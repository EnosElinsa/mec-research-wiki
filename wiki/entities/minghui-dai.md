# Minghui Dai

Affiliated with the **State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City, Department of Computer and Information Science, University of Macau** (Macau, China; email `minghuidai@um.edu.mo`). Received the Ph.D. from Shanghai University in 2021; per the parse, a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Macau, with research interests in wireless network architecture and vehicular networks — consistent across his papers in this wiki.

Co-authored 4 sources in this wiki, all on marine edge computing:

- [[dai-2024-multiuav-marine-welfare]] — multi-UAV multi-access marine MEC with a double-auction OBS-selection design (system-welfare / energy) (lead author).
- [[dai-2023-hybrid-marine-mmwl]] — hybrid offshore (FDMA) + aerial-UAV (NOMA) multi-access offloading for marine networks (MMWL) (lead author).
- [[dai-2023-hybrid-noma-fdma-marine]] — hybrid NOMA-underwater (USN→USV) + FDMA-aerial (USV→UAV) multi-access offloading; total-energy minimization + secrecy provisioning against an eavesdropper (lead author).
- [[zeng-2024-usv-fleet-collaborative-offloading]] — USV fleet-assisted collaborative computation offloading with a reverse-auction incentive (co-author).
