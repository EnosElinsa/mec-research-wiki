# Yuguang Fang

IEEE Fellow. Affiliated with the **Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong** (Hong Kong SAR, China; email `my.fang@cityu.edu.hk`) across his papers in this wiki. (Per his in-parse bio: Ph.D. degrees from Case Western Reserve University and Boston University; formerly at the University of Florida.)

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki, spanning maritime MEC and UAV-enabled Computing Power Networks:

- [[wang-2024-maritime-eh-jcora]] — energy-harvesting maritime MEC sea-lane monitoring (Lyapunov / JCORA).
- [[qi-2024-msar-minmax-latency]] — multi-UAV maritime search-and-rescue min-max-latency offloading (S-UAV/R-UAV).
- [[deng-2026-uav-cpn-energy]] — UAV-enabled Computing Power Network analysis under communication/computation latency and UAV energy constraints.

## Contributions to this wiki

- A senior co-author in the **Dalian-Maritime-University maritime cluster** led by [[bin-lin]], appearing on two maritime offloading papers alongside [[zhen-wang]] / [[qiang-ye]] (energy-harvesting JCORA) and the Qi/Deng/Chen group (MSAR min-max latency), plus an analytical [[uav-enabled-computing-power-network]] entry.
