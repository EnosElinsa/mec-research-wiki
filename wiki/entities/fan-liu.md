# Fan Liu

IEEE Senior Member whose parses record a move from Southern University of Science and Technology to a professorship at Southeast University's National Mobile Communications Research Laboratory. The 2024 UAV-ISAC parse lists `liuf6@sustech.edu.cn`, B.Eng. and Ph.D. degrees from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2013 and 2018, and earlier visiting/research appointments at University College London.
