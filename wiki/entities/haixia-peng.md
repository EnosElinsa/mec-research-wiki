# Haixia Peng

IEEE Member. Per her in-parse bios: first Ph.D. (computer science) from Northeastern University, China (2017), second Ph.D. (electrical and computer engineering) from the **University of Waterloo** (2021). On the 2020 UAV-vehicular paper she is at the University of Waterloo (`h27peng@uwaterloo.ca`); by the 2024 satellite-marine paper she is a Full Professor at the **School of Information and Communications Engineering, Xi'an Jiaotong University** (`haixia.peng@xjtu.edu.cn`) — a move, consistent identity.

Co-authored 2 sources in this wiki:

- [[peng-2020-maddpg-uav-vehicular]] — MADDPG resource management in MEC- and UAV-assisted vehicular networks (corresponding author; University of Waterloo).
- [[wang-2024-twotier-satellite-marine]] — two-tier task offloading for satellite-assisted marine networks (Xi'an Jiaotong University).

## Contributions to this wiki
