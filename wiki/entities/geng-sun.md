Co-authored 5 sources in this wiki, spanning vehicular, post-disaster, energy-efficient, secure-communication, and survey work in aerial/edge networks:

- [[sun-2023-bargain-match-vec]] — bargaining + matching for vehicular edge computing.
- [[sun-2024-mvtora-postdisaster-vfc]] — post-disaster aerial-terrestrial MEC + vehicle fog computing.

- [[zhang-2024-gdmtd3-aerial-secure-cb]] — UAV-swarm secure collaborative beamforming via diffusion-enhanced TD3.
