# Yuben Qu

IEEE Member. Affiliated with **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)** and the **Key Laboratory of Dynamic Cognitive System of Electromagnetic Spectrum Space, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology** (Nanjing, China); email `quyuben@nuaa.edu.cn` — consistent across his papers in this wiki.

Co-authored 2 sources in this wiki, both on in-swarm collaborative DL inference:

- [[sun-2024-asap-uav-swarm]] — ASAP all-sky autonomous collaborative computing in a UAV swarm.
