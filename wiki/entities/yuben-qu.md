# Yuben Qu

IEEE Member. Affiliated with **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)** and the **Key Laboratory of Dynamic Cognitive System of Electromagnetic Spectrum Space, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology** (Nanjing, China); email `quyuben@nuaa.edu.cn` — consistent across his papers in this wiki.

Co-authored 3 sources in this wiki across collaborative UAV inference and visual monitoring:

- [[qu-ecoei-uav-swarm]] — eCoEI elastic collaborative edge intelligence architecture for UAV swarms (corresponding author).
- [[sun-2024-asap-uav-swarm]] — ASAP all-sky autonomous collaborative computing in a UAV swarm.
- [[zhang-2026-omnidirectional-monitoring-deployment]] — joint UAV and fixed-camera deployment for continuous omnidirectional monitoring.

## Contributions to this wiki

- A lead contributor to the NUAA **UAV-swarm collaborative-inference** thread, working alongside [[hao-sun]], [[chao-dong]], and [[qihui-wu]] (with Song Guo and [[jiawen-kang]] on eCoEI), and a contributor to heterogeneous UAV/camera monitoring deployment.
