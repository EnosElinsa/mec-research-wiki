# Jianping Yao

Researcher with the School of Information Engineering, Guangdong University of Technology (`yaojp@gdut.edu.cn`), also listed with the Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Future Networks of Intelligence at CUHK Shenzhen. The GDUT address and email recur across the 2025 and conference bylines, and the 2026 biography reports a GDUT B.E. and South China University of Technology M.E. and Ph.D., which supports one identity.
