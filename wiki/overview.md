# Overview

A long-running research wiki on **mobile edge computing (MEC)** broadly construed — task offloading, resource allocation, trajectory and infrastructure design, and intelligent decision-making algorithms across UAV, HAPS, LEO-satellite, vehicular, maritime, and terrestrial deployments. Open-ended scope; sources accumulate as the corpus grows.

## Tracks emerging from the corpus

Cross-track entries worth noting: [[liu-2026-lyapunov-diffusion-uav-vehicular]] connects vehicular MEC, Lyapunov control, delayed CSI, and diffusion-actor DRL; [[xu-2026-prizty-uav-mec-auction]] adds a privacy-preserving auction mechanism for UAV-MEC; [[li-2023-adaptive-digital-twin-uav-iscc]], [[he-2026-dt-sagimec-lae]], and [[li-2025-dt-uav-swarm-resource-management]] link digital twins to ISCC, low-altitude SAGIMEC, and UAV-swarm task-driven resource management; [[shi-2026-aoi-active-ris-noma-agmec]] extends AoI-driven MEC into active-RIS/NOMA air-ground networks; [[wei-2026-airfogsim-uav-vfc]] adds simulator infrastructure for UAV-integrated vehicle fog computing.

Additional cross-track entries include [[chen-2026-cargo-uav-pickup-lae]] for cellular-connected cargo-UAV logistics, [[huang-2026-coded-caching-uav-marine]] for coded caching and AUV D2D content delivery, [[zhou-2026-cpsfl-uav-foundation-models]] for split federated foundation-model fine-tuning in UAV networks, [[qi-2026-drone-vehicle-mec-inspection]] for vehicle-carried drone MEC inspection, and [[tang-2025-cooperative-isac-lae]] for cooperative multi-BS ISAC sensing in LAE.

Further cross-track entries include [[zhang-2026-uav-task-path-lu-its]] for low-altitude ITS task allocation plus collision-free path planning, [[huang-2026-amappo-satellite-edge]] for asynchronous DAG-aware UAV-assisted satellite edge computing, [[panahi-2026-uav-green-iot-offloading]] for energy-procurement compensation in green IoT offloading, [[chen-2026-dart-hap-uav-mec]] for Lyapunov-guided DDPG-attention in HAP-UAV-MEC, and [[jia-2026-ufsp-rail-inspection]] for imperfect-information fictitious self-play in rail-line UAV inspection.

Semantic, demand-aware, and robustness entries cut across several tracks: [[wang-2026-lifelong-semantic-content-reuse]] links Metaverse edge rendering, [[semantic-content-reuse]], caching, and lifelong learning; [[wang-2026-diffusion-semantic-uav-edge]] connects semantic edge computing to diffusion-actor trajectory optimization; [[zhai-2026-collaborative-inference-uav-mec]] joins DNN split inference with multi-UAV MEC; [[zhou-2021-delay-sagin-task-scheduling]] adds constrained risk-sensitive RL to the SAGIN task-scheduling lineage; [[peng-2026-demand-aware-multiuav-mec]] raises the CMOP lineage to demand-aware multi-area fleet allocation; and [[jia-2026-dro-lawn-trajectory]] expands DRO from CSI-error robustness into task-size-distribution robustness with UAV trajectory control.

The corpus also covers stochastic-game UAV-MEC offloading/deployment ([[ning-2023-uav-mec-offloading-deployment]]), edge intelligence in ITS ([[gong-2023-edge-intelligence-its-survey]]), UAV-mounted STAR-RIS NOMA MEC ([[mohammadi-2026-star-ris-uav-mec-noma]]), RIS-assisted UAV-USV inland-waterway MEC ([[liao-2025-ris-uav-usv-resource-allocation]]), and hierarchical DRL over uneven-terrain UAV-MEC ([[tong-2026-uneven-terrain-uav-mec]]).

Low-altitude, semantic, and green-AEC entries connect several tracks: [[wang-2026-blockchain-lae-fl-mappo]] ties blockchain-assisted FL/MAPPO to low-altitude offloading, caching, queueing, and PV throttling; [[zhao-2025-probabilistic-semantic-sagin]] adds [[probabilistic-semantic-communication]] to the SAGIN energy-efficiency thread; [[xiao-2025-star-ris-bidirectional-uav-mec]] extends STAR-RIS UAV-MEC into same-slot bidirectional offloading; [[wang-2026-secure-lae-uav-scheduling]] adds secure LAE communication with dynamic communication/jamming UAV roles; and [[ma-2026-mean-field-green-aec]] brings [[mean-field-game]] task allocation to green aerial edge computing for metaverse users.

Secure, storage, identification, and generalizable-offloading entries broaden the same tracks: [[wang-2026-secure-reliable-uav-mec]] adds artificial-noise PLS and secrecy-outage reliability inside UAV-MEC energy-efficiency optimization; [[li-2025-energy-latency-uav-vec]] connects UAV-assisted VEC to FL participant selection; [[huang-2026-erasure-coded-uav-storage]] adds [[erasure-coded-edge-storage]] to post-disaster UAV edge systems; [[zeng-2026-fmcw-isibc-lae]] connects FMCW sensing with UAV backscatter identification; and [[yang-2025-generalizable-pareto-offloading]] adds context-conditioned MORL for Pareto MEC offloading.

Channel-estimation, sequential-offloading, HSD, vehicle-twin, and HybridRAG entries connect the LAE, multi-UAV-MEC, vehicular-metaverse, and generative-AI tracks: [[yang-2026-generative-radio-map-lae]] uses CVCGAN-generated radio maps for LAE channel estimation; [[teng-2026-gstrl-sequential-offloading]] turns order-constrained task offloading into graph-based PPO; [[zhao-2026-heuristic-supervised-drl]] formalizes heuristic-supervised DRL with a PSO-MARL UAV-MEC case study; [[chen-2026-hc-mappo-vehicle-twin-migration]] treats vehicle-twin migration as UAV-assisted vehicular-metaverse service continuity; and [[wen-2026-hybridrag-low-carbon-lae]] combines HybridRAG problem formulation with diffusion-enhanced SAC for low-carbon LAE optimization.

LAE-ISAC and VEC-ISAC entries now add sensing-aware control to both airspace and road-hotspot settings: [[ye-2026-deeplsc-lae-isac]] and [[ye-2026-meta-deepesc-lae-isac]] treat GBS beamforming plus authorized-UAV trajectories as episode-level DRL control, while [[li-2026-isac-vec-beamforming-deployment]] couples UAV deployment and beamforming for ISAC-enhanced vehicular edge coverage.

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Lyapunov + DRL hybrids are still common** for long-term-constrained per-slot MEC optimization ([[qin-2025-bcuav-masac]], [[zhu-2025-lycnn-drl-wpt-mec]], [[gao-2024-sagin-perception-offloading]], [[qin-2025-matd3-noma-queue-sagin]], [[you-2025-uncertain-maritime-hasac]], [[zhou-2024-jdl-abs-postdisaster-rescue]]) — the pattern is mapped in the [[lyapunov-guided-drl]] methodology page. See also the alternating-optimization (AO + SDR + SCA) version in [[benaya-2025-aerial-isac-haps]].
2. **CTDE remains a recurring multi-agent paradigm** ([[peng-2025-drudm-cfg]], [[wang-2026-blockchain-lae-fl-mappo]], [[zhang-2025-mcma-task-migration]], [[kang-2023-mappo-hierarchical-aerial]]), now mapped as the [[ctde-multi-agent-drl-protocol]]; [[zhang-2025-ssac-mgi-heterogeneous-uav]] is the explicit DTDE contrast case, and [[qin-2025-bcuav-masac]] is a related MASAC / Lyapunov hybrid rather than a parse-labeled CTDE source.
3. **Game models keep broadening** — Stackelberg + matching keeps showing up ([[wang-2025-uav-swarm-stackelberg]], [[bi-2025-sg-mapg]], [[wang-2026-aerial-marine-msar]] adds many-to-one matching, [[nabi-2025-jour-hierarchical-aerial]] adds Gale-Shapley), while [[ma-2026-mean-field-green-aec]] adds [[mean-field-game]] modeling for large-population UAV energy/task allocation.
4. **Two-stage decomposition (discrete-then-continuous) is a recurring solver pattern** — [[wang-2026-aerial-marine-msar]], [[nabi-2025-jour-hierarchical-aerial]], [[jia-2025-dro-uav-hap-mec]]. Compare with the **joint hybrid-action** family ([[liu-2026-jppo-en-convntm|j-PPO]], [[ma-2025-pdqn-vehicular-mec|P-DQN]]), now compared head-to-head in [[j-ppo-vs-pdqn]]. See [[two-stage-decomposition]] and the [[discrete-continuous-two-stage-decomposition]] solver-protocol methodology page.
5. **Fairness metrics fragment** — Jain, Theil, and now energy-balancing variance ([[huang-2023-mu-aec-task-energy]], [[nabi-2025-jour-hierarchical-aerial]]) and completion-time difference ([[peng-2024-energy-time-uav-its]]). The [[fairness-metrics-in-mec]] page now collects these side by side.
6. **Uncertainty is an explicit concern** in several different ways: distributionally robust CSI-error control ([[jia-2025-dro-uav-hap-mec]]), task-size-distribution DRO ([[jia-2026-dro-lawn-trajectory]]), known-route side-step ([[wang-2026-aerial-marine-msar]]), terrain-aware geometric modeling ([[wu-2026-terrain-aware-uav-mec]]), and uneven-terrain safe-flight/service-coverage learning ([[tong-2026-uneven-terrain-uav-mec]]) — these sit alongside hard-safety and bounded-robust mechanisms in the [[safety-and-robustness-mechanisms-in-mec]] synthesis.
7. **DRL is not the only game in town.** The **evolutionary / classical** branch is comparable in size to the DRL branch, which justifies the [[drl-vs-evolutionary-vs-classical-solvers]] synthesis.
8. **Most papers are still simulation-only or emulation-only.** Only 4 of the 305 curated sources have real hardware / real-world deployment evidence ([[sun-2024-asap-uav-swarm]], [[shao-2024-drl-antijamming-mec]], [[zhang-2020-response-delay-uav-swarm]], [[jia-2026-ufsp-rail-inspection]]); [[qu-ecoei-uav-swarm]] adds a **proof-of-concept** prototype (4 real airborne Jetson devices), [[ma-2026-mean-field-green-aec]] adds Raspberry Pi5/A100 hardware-in-the-loop emulation, [[sun-2024-imssa-uav-secure-cb]] a Raspberry Pi practicality demonstration, [[bai-2024-delay-aware-cooperative-edge-cloud]] a real UAV-edge platform used to *verify its model* (not a full algorithm deployment), and [[wei-2026-airfogsim-uav-vfc]] adds reusable simulator infrastructure. The rungs of validation depth and the sim-to-real challenges the sources name are mapped in [[hardware-validation-and-sim-to-real-in-mec]]. Worth keeping in mind for any thesis claim.
