# Emerging Technologies in Intelligent Metasurfaces: Shaping the Future of Wireless Communications

## Citation

An, J., Debbah, M., Cui, T. J., Chen, Z. N., & Yuen, C. (2026). *Emerging Technologies in Intelligent Metasurfaces: Shaping the Future of Wireless Communications*. **IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation, 74**(5). DOI: 10.1109/TAP.2025.3571069.

## TL;DR

This survey reviews programmable 2-D reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, 3-D stacked intelligent metasurfaces for wave-domain analog processing, and shape-morphable flexible intelligent metasurfaces. It combines reported prototypes, models, application demonstrations, and open integration challenges.

## Related Work Paragraph

An et al. [x] surveyed intelligent metasurfaces across programmable RIS, stacked intelligent metasurfaces, and flexible intelligent metasurfaces. The review covers RIS channel modeling, field trials, and wireless applications, then explains how stacked layers implement analog electromagnetic processing and how flexible surfaces adapt their physical shape to changing channels. Reported examples include RIS prototype gains, wave-domain beamforming and sensing, and flexible-surface diversity and transmit-power benefits. The numerical values summarize prior prototypes and case studies rather than a new optimization result from this survey. Open issues include aperture and layer loss, nonlinear processing, CSI over continuous shapes, actuation energy, and deployment durability.

## Scope and method

The article organizes prior experiments, mathematical models, prototypes, application scenarios, and numerical illustrations for RIS, SIM, and FIM technologies. It does not define one decision, reward, and feasible-domain tuple for an optimization problem.

## Key findings

- RIS prototypes demonstrate field-trial gains in received power and SNR in the reviewed studies.
- SIMs process electromagnetic signals in the wave domain without the digital storage and preprocessing used by conventional pipelines.
- FIMs use shape morphing to adapt antenna geometry to dynamic wireless channels.

## Limitations / future work

The survey identifies unresolved limits in SIM scaling, nonlinear processing, continuous-space CSI, actuator energy, hardware robustness, and deployment durability.
