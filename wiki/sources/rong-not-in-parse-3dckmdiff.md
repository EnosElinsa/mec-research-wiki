# 3DCKMDiff: 3D CKM Construction Based on Generative Diffusion for Environment-Aware Low-Altitude Communication and Sensing

## Citation

Rong, H., Zeng, Y., Xu, X., Li, R., & Xu, J. *3DCKMDiff: 3D CKM Construction Based on Generative Diffusion for Environment-Aware Low-Altitude Communication and Sensing*. Venue, year, and DOI are not in the parse.

## TL;DR

Low-altitude UAV operations need channel knowledge across 3-D space, but measurements are sparse and arrive from a few height slices. 3DCKMDiff treats complete 3-D channel knowledge map (CKM) construction as a 3-D inverse problem and solves it with a 2-D generative diffusion model that learns the joint distribution of multiple slices, outperforming 3-D Kriging interpolation.

## Related Work Paragraph

> Ready to reuse in a literature review. Replace `[x]` with the formal citation number.

Rong et al. [x] studied channel knowledge map construction for environment-aware communication and sensing in UAV-assisted low-altitude applications. They noted that in low-altitude environments the CKM must be extended from a two-dimensional plane to three-dimensional space and completed from sparsely sampled data on different slices of the flight region. They formulated the task as a 3D inverse problem and proposed 3DCKMDiff, which uses a 2D generative diffusion model to learn the joint distribution of multiple slices and thereby gain 3D prior knowledge. Simulation results show that, compared with benchmark methods such as 3D Kriging interpolation, the method improves CKM construction accuracy and robustness, and it can infer the CKM even at height levels with no sampled data.

## Scope and method

The method reconstructs a map from sparse measurements and is evaluated with PSNR, SSIM, NMSE, and RMSE on simulated datasets, including data generated by Sionna. It is a channel-map estimation method and defines no decision variables, objective, or constraints for network control.

## Key findings

- On Sionna-generated data, 3DCKMDiff reaches a PSNR of 46.41 dB, SSIM of 0.9614, and NMSE of 0.0052.
- It achieves lower RMSE and NMSE than 3D Kriging interpolation on both datasets.
- It reconstructs slices at heights with no sampling data.

## Limitations / future work

Evaluation uses simulated environments.
